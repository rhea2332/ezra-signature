Email signature for The Ezra Clinic

Check the preview, copy it, then paste it into your email signature settings.

Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings, then See all settings, then General, and scroll to Signature.
  3. Create or select a signature, paste into the box, then click Save changes at the bottom of the page.

Outlook (web and new Outlook)

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook, open Settings, then Mail (or Account), then Signatures.
  3. Paste into the signature box, then click Save.
